About CoffeeGuard

👨‍🎓 About the Creator

Tumusiime Innocent is an Actuarial Science graduate from Makerere University (Class of 2026). He built CoffeeGuard as part of his dissertation research on applying AI to improve coffee farming in Uganda.

☕ What Does CoffeeGuard Do?

CoffeeGuard uses AI to detect diseases on coffee leaves. Just upload a photo, and CoffeeGuard identifies Brown Eye Spot, Leaf Miner, Leaf Rust, or Red Spider Mite.

📱 How to Use

1. Take a clear photo of your coffee leaves
2. Upload it to CoffeeGuard
3. Get instant disease detection with confidence scores
4. Follow the AI recommendations to protect your crop
