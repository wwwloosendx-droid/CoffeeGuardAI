About CoffeeGuard

👨‍🎓 About the Creator

Tumusiime Innocent is an Actuarial Science graduate from Makerere University (Class of 2026). He built CoffeeGuard as part of his dissertation research on applying artificial intelligence to improve coffee farming in Uganda. His work focuses on using computer vision to help farmers accurately determine coffee cherry ripeness, reducing harvest waste and increasing income.

☕ What Does CoffeeGuard Do?

CoffeeGuard is a smart farming tool that uses artificial intelligence to check if your coffee cherries are ready to pick. Just take a photo with your phone, upload it, and CoffeeGuard tells you if they are ripe, still ripening, or not yet ready. This helps you harvest at the right time and get better quality coffee.

📱 How to Use CoffeeGuard

1. Take a clear photo of your coffee cherries
2. Upload it to CoffeeGuard (AI automatically verifies coffee content)
3. Get instant results on ripeness with confidence scores
4. Use this information to plan your harvest schedule
5. Track your predictions over time to improve your farming decisions

🌍 Why Uganda Coffee?

Coffee is Uganda's biggest export, supporting over 5 million families across the country. Despite this, many farmers struggle to know when coffee is ready to pick, leading to reduced quality and income. CoffeeGuard brings expert knowledge to every farmer's pocket, helping them earn more from their coffee and contribute to Uganda's economy.
