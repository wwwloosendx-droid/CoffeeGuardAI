About CoffeeGuard

👨‍🌾 Who Made This?

Tumusiime Innocent is a student at Makerere University studying Actuarial Science. He built CoffeeGuard to help farmers like you use technology to grow better coffee.

☕ What Does CoffeeGuard Do?

CoffeeGuard is a smart farming tool that uses artificial intelligence to check if your coffee cherries are ready to pick. Just take a photo, and it tells you if they are ripe, still ripening, or not yet ready. This helps you harvest at the right time and get better quality coffee.

📱 How to Use It

1. Take a photo of your coffee cherries
2. Upload it to CoffeeGuard
3. Get instant results on ripeness
4. Use this info to plan your harvest

🌍 Why Uganda Coffee?

Coffee is Uganda's biggest export and supports millions of families. But many farmers struggle to know when coffee is ready to pick. CoffeeGuard gives you expert knowledge right on your phone, helping you earn more money from your coffee.
